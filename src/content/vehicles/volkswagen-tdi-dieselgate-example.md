## Why It Is Included

This page is included as a pointer to the Dieselgate controversy, not as a review of any specific model. The historically relevant subject is the interaction between diesel emissions hardware, engine control software, laboratory certification, and real-world operation.

## Technologies Illustrated

Affected Volkswagen TDI vehicles are useful examples for studying diesel combustion, NOx control, aftertreatment, ECU calibration, onboard diagnostics, and regulatory definitions of defeat devices.
