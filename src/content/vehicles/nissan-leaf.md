## Why It Is Included

The Nissan Leaf is included as an example of a mass-market battery electric vehicle. It helps readers study how a vehicle changes when the traction system is based on a battery, inverter, motor, and charging interface rather than a combustion engine.

## Technologies Illustrated

The vehicle illustrates BEV packaging, traction batteries, onboard charging, regenerative braking, and the importance of charging infrastructure.
