## Why It Is Included

The Mercedes-Benz 260 D is included as a useful example of early diesel passenger-car application. It helps separate diesel history from the later emissions and software controversies that shaped public discussion decades afterward.

## Technologies Illustrated

The vehicle illustrates compression ignition, diesel fuel use, and the adaptation of engine technology associated with commercial vehicles into passenger-car packaging.
