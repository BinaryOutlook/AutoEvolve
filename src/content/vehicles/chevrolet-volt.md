## Why It Is Included

The Chevrolet Volt is included to explain the transition from conventional hybrids toward plug-in electric-drive vehicles. It is a useful example of how battery size, charging access, and backup liquid-fuel operation can be combined.

## Technologies Illustrated

The vehicle illustrates charge-depleting operation, onboard charging, traction batteries larger than conventional hybrid packs, and control strategies that decide when the combustion engine is used.
