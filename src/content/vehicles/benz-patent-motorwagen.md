## Why It Is Included

The Benz Patent-Motorwagen is included as an example of early gasoline automobile integration. Its value for this site is not as a review subject, but as a way to study how engine, chassis, steering, and drivetrain concepts began to converge.

## Technologies Illustrated

The vehicle illustrates the shift from isolated engine experiments toward vehicle-level packaging. It helps readers connect piston engines, fuel storage, ignition, and road-vehicle controls.
