## Why It Is Included

The Ford Model T is included to show how manufacturing and standardization shaped automotive history. Its role is not limited to any single component; it illustrates how production systems can make a technology socially and economically visible.

## Technologies Illustrated

The Model T connects gasoline engines, carburetion, simple vehicle controls, serviceability, and manufacturing scale. It helps explain why automobile history depends on factories as well as engines.
