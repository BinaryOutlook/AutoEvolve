## Definition

A catalytic converter is an exhaust device that promotes chemical reactions without being consumed by those reactions. In gasoline vehicles, three-way catalysts reduce carbon monoxide, unburned hydrocarbons, and NOx when the engine is controlled near stoichiometric mixture.

## System Role

The converter depends on upstream control. Oxygen sensors and the engine control unit regulate the air-fuel mixture so the catalyst can operate effectively.

## Historical Role

Catalytic converters linked emissions regulation to engine electronics. They helped move gasoline vehicles from mechanical fuel control toward sensor-based feedback systems.
