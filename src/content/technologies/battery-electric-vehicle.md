## Definition

A battery electric vehicle, or BEV, uses stored electrical energy for propulsion and has no onboard combustion engine for traction.

## Energy Storage

A simplified battery energy relationship is:

$$
E = V \times Q
$$

where \(E\) is energy, \(V\) is voltage, and \(Q\) is charge capacity.

## System Role

BEVs depend on the coordinated operation of a traction battery, battery management system, inverter, electric motor, onboard charger, DC/DC converter, thermal system, and charging interface.
