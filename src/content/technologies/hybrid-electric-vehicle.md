## Definition

A hybrid electric vehicle combines an internal combustion engine with one or more electric machines and a traction battery. The vehicle may use the engine, the motor, or both depending on operating conditions.

## Energy Flow

Regenerative braking allows some kinetic energy to return to the battery:

$$
E_\text{recovered} < \frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$

The inequality matters because conversion losses, tire grip, battery limits, and braking demand prevent full recovery.

## Historical Role

Hybrids showed that electrification did not have to replace combustion immediately. Electric machines could first be used to improve how combustion vehicles operated.
