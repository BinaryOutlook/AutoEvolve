## Historical Context

Postwar automobile production expanded with rising highway investment, industrial capacity, and consumer demand. Vehicles became more comfortable, faster, and easier to operate. The automobile also became deeply tied to land use, commuting patterns, fuel supply, and national infrastructure.

## Engineering Changes

Automatic transmissions, more refined suspension systems, radial tires, and unibody construction changed vehicle behavior and manufacturing. Engine architectures diversified across regions and vehicle classes.

## What Remained Unresolved

Safety and emissions were not yet controlled with the same intensity that later decades would bring. The technical foundation was strong, but the public consequences of scale were becoming harder to ignore.
